N₂ enters at the top, accelerates through the throat, and expands along the nozzle. Collisions hold ¹⁴N₂, ¹⁴N¹⁵N, and ¹⁵N₂ to one directed speed. At freeze-out the collisions stop. The lighter molecules keep a slightly larger sideways speed, so they arrive at wider angles. The skimmer retains the gas near the axis, where the heavier isotopologues are enriched.

The center cut can be sent through the same nozzle again. Each stage multiplies the enrichment. The curves below the skimmer are the arrival angles after the number of stages set on the right, drawn to a common peak. Their widths follow the ¹⁵N₂ fraction in that product. The molecules in the nozzle show a single stage.
